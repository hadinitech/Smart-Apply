What does the SmartApply fee cover?

Your SmartApply fee pays for our assistance service — helping you get your application right, not for the application itself.

It can cover

  • Helping you understand the application process
  • Checking the information you provide for completeness
  • Assisting with supported application forms
  • Guiding you through required documents
  • Assisting with application submission
  • Communicating relevant submission information to you

It does not cover

  • Fees charged by universities or colleges
  • Admission or registration decisions
  • Guaranteed acceptance into any programme
