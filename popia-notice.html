POPIA Notice

SmartApply processes personal information in accordance with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

We process personal information only where it is necessary for the services we provide, and we take reasonable steps to ensure it is protected and used lawfully.

Purpose of processing

  • To assess your application needs.
  • To communicate with you about supported institutions and application steps.
  • To provide assistance related to your selected application package.

Security

We apply reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected and in line with any legal or operational obligations.

To exercise your rights under POPIA or request more information, contact SmartApply directly.
