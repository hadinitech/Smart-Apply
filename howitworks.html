Get ready before you chat

Before you start, have these ready

Depending on the institution, you may need the following. Don't worry if it's not all in hand yet.

Identity

Your South African ID or relevant identification document.

Academic information

Your latest matric results, statement of results or other academic records.

Contact details

Your phone number, email address and residential information.

Programme choice

The course or programme you would like to apply for.

Supporting documents

Additional documents may be required depending on the institution or programme.

Don't have everything ready yet? That's okay. Chat with us and we'll explain what you need.
