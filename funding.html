6 funding options

NSFAS

NSFAS Bursary

Government bursary

Funding for approved studies at South African public universities and TVET colleges. Covers tuition and approved study and living allowances.

Who it is forFinancial and academic criteria apply. The usual household income limit is R350 000 per year, or R600 000 for applicants with disabilities.
View funding
DEPARTMENT OF BASIC EDUCATION

Funza Lushaka Bursary

Teaching bursary

Funding for initial teacher education in priority teaching subjects. Recipients commit to teaching at a public school for the same number of years they receive funding.

Who it is forFirst-time applicants must be 30 or younger and meet admission and bursary selection criteria for an eligible teaching qualification.
View funding
ISFAP

ISFAP Bursaries

University bursary

Financial assistance and student support for selected university qualifications linked to occupations in high demand. Available study options depend on the funder and institution.

Who it is forApplicants undergo financial means testing and academic selection. Check the supported qualifications and universities on the official application portal.
View funding
SASOL

Sasol Mainstream Bursary

Engineering & science bursary

Support for selected engineering, science and technology degrees, including tuition, accommodation, meals and study allowances. Work or repayment obligations apply.

Who it is forSouth African citizens under 30 pursuing eligible full-time degrees at approved universities. Subject and academic requirements vary by qualification.
View funding
INVESTEC / STUDYTRUST

Investec Tertiary Bursary

Science & financial sector bursary

A full bursary towards study expenses for science and financial sector degrees at local universities, with academic, personal and career support.

Who it is forYoung South African citizens with financial need and academic potential. Published school criteria include 70% in English and Mathematics and 60% in other subjects.
View funding
ALLAN GRAY ORBIS FOUNDATION

Allan Gray Fellowship

University fellowship

University funding based on financial need, with mentoring and entrepreneurial development at partner universities. Funding levels depend on household circumstances.

Who it is forGrade 12 South African applicants under 21 with strong academic results and entrepreneurial potential. Check eligible degrees, partner universities and the next intake.
View funding
