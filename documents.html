Documents You May Need

Before starting an application, it is helpful to have your important documents ready.

Your preparation checklist

Keep these documents close

Depending on the institution and your circumstances, you may be asked for the following.

01 / IDENTITY

Identification document

A clear copy of your South African ID or relevant identification document.

02 / ACADEMICS

Latest academic results

Your latest statement of results, school report or academic record.

03 / FINAL RESULTS

Matric certificate or final results

Have your certificate or final results available when they are issued.

04 / HOUSEHOLD

Parent or guardian information

Some applications may request parent or guardian contact and personal details.

05 / RESIDENCE

Proof of address

A recent document confirming your residential address, where required.

06 / SUPPORT

Proof of income

Financial documents may be needed for funding, residence or specific applications.

Important: Requirements are not the same for every institution or application. Always check the official institution's requirements before submitting.
A useful habit

Make your documents easier to use

  • Keep clear digital copies in a labelled folder on your phone or computer.
  • Check that scans and photos are readable before uploading them.
  • Keep supporting documents requested by the specific institution together.
  • Never send sensitive documents to unofficial websites or unknown contacts.
Tip: Clear digital copies make online applications quicker and easier to complete.
